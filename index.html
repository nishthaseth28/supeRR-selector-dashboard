These scores are supporting evidence, not the formula that generated the final player order. The shortlist also reflects broader judgement such as overall record, role, wickets and team impact.

How to interpret the dashboard

The final order is a multi-factor selection judgement. Pressure-performance data is shown as a cross-check; it should not be read as a pure statistical ranking.

Sample-size caution

Players flagged “below 150-ball minimum” have limited pressure-ball samples for stable comparison. Treat their pressure scores cautiously.

Coverage caution

Nathan Bracken is not present in the dataset used for this dashboard. Players with no pressure data are not plotted in the evidence charts.

Comparability

IPL and T20I contexts differ. The deck also notes that opposition and batting-position adjustments were not included.

Reliability

The presentation reports modest split-half rank correlations (batters 0.17; bowlers 0.09), indicating pressure form is noisy and only modestly repeatable.
